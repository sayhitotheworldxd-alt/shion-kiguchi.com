About

Trained as a physician in Japan, Shion spent six years in clinical surgery and residency before moving into global pharmaceutical R&D at Pfizer, where she led cross-functional clinical trial strategy across dermatology, respiratory, and immunology programs.

She is currently an MBA candidate at the Haas School of Business at UC Berkeley, building on a decade of clinical and pharmaceutical leadership to move into broader healthcare strategy and business roles.
